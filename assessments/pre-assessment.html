Pre-assessment

This is not a test you can fail. It does two jobs: it puts you on the right track for the next nine weeks, and it records where the cohort started so that on 31 October we can prove what changed. Answer honestly — guessing well now only lands you in a track that will frustrate you. About 20 minutes. Closes Sunday 6 September.
