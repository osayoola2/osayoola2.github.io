Programme Overview

Welcome to the Practical AI Fellowship

A nine-week self-paced programme taking 3MTT fellows through Meta's AI curriculum on Coursera and DeepLearning.AI, from first principles to a working project of your own.

DurationNine weeks · 1 September to 31 October 2026. Around five to seven hours a week.
FormatSelf-paced study on Coursera and DeepLearning.AI, guided week by week, with cohort support on WhatsApp.
CostFree. Every course in the programme is accessible at no cost with a free account on either platform.
Delivered byRobotics and Artificial Intelligence Nigeria (RAIN), on behalf of 3MTT and Meta.

About the course material

Every course is Meta's own — Meta's Coursera catalogue, plus three short courses built with Meta and taught by their partner engineering team on DeepLearning.AI. Nothing in the required path sits behind a subscription.

How It Works

Everyone starts together, then splits three ways. The first three weeks are the same for every fellow, whatever your background. After that you follow the path matched to how you work, and the last two weeks are for building something of your own.

Shared foundation What generative AI is really doing, where it fails, and why the data underneath decides the quality of everything on top. No coding, nothing assumed. Weeks 1–3 · all fellows
Your own track Business analysis without code, applied Python for data, or building directly on Meta's Llama models. The pre-assessment recommends one; you can move if it's wrong. Weeks 4–7 · by track
Something real Every fellow finishes by building one thing on a problem from their own work or community, and explaining honestly what it does and does not do. Weeks 8–9 · capstone

Choose Your Track

Pick one to load your schedule. You can change it whenever you like, and moving down a track after an honest attempt is normal — finishing a track beats stalling in a harder one.

How Progress Is Measured

Every assessment in the fellowship is written and scored by RAIN on Google Forms. The learning platforms do not provide graded quizzes on free access and do not report scores, so all measurement is ours. The pre-assessment in Week 1 and the post-assessment in Week 9 carry the same thirty questions — the difference between them is the impact number.

What You Need To Finish

Free access to these courses does not include platform certificates, so completion here is evidenced by your own work and your assessment record rather than by a certificate you would have to pay for.
