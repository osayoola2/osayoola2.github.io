Programme Overview

The widest entry point into the programme

AI Skills Development is open to the full 3MTT community — developers, students and early-career professionals. The priority is reach: getting practical AI skills to as many people across Nigeria as possible, with nothing to pay.

DurationNine weeks · 1 September to 31 October 2026. Around four to six hours a week.
Two delivery formatsSelf-paced modules you complete on your own schedule, plus scheduled virtual instructor-led sessions for those who want more structure.
ContentAI 101 — 20.5 hours across four modules — plus the Meta Blueprint courses. AI 101 is compulsory for every participant.
CostFree. Every required course is accessible at no cost with a free account.

🏆 Scholarships for top performers

AI 101 Curriculum

Four modules, 20.5 hours, developed by Startupbootcamp for Meta. Every participant works through all four — this is the compulsory spine of the programme, and the four module assessments are what decide the top 100. Slides for each section are downloadable from your schedule once you log in.

Meta Blueprint Courses

The curated set named in the programme brief, taken alongside AI 101. These sit on Coursera and DeepLearning.AI and are free to access — Coursera in audit mode, DeepLearning.AI's videos on the free tier.

Optional extension

Not required and not counted toward completion, but here if you want to go further.

How Progress Is Measured

Every assessment is written and scored by RAIN on Google Forms. The learning platforms do not provide graded quizzes on free access and do not report scores back, so all measurement is ours. The pre-assessment in Week 1 and the post-assessment in Week 9 share the same questions — the difference between them is the impact number. The four module assessments in between are what rank the cohort for scholarships.

What You Need To Finish

Free access to these courses does not include platform certificates, so completion is evidenced by your own work and your assessment record rather than by a certificate you would have to pay for.
